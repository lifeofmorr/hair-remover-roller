Does it work on clothes?

It is aimed at furniture, bedding, carpets and car seats. For clothing, short strokes on sturdy fabrics work best; delicate knits are better served by a gentler brush.

What about high-pile carpet?

The source report notes it is not a vacuum replacement and will not pull deeply embedded hair out of high-pile carpet. Use it for surface fur on low-pile fabrics.
